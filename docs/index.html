About this catalogue

Every piece here is stocked in the workshop and shipped from it. We list weight, dimensions and material on each product page because those are the numbers that decide whether a thing fits your room, and we would rather you knew before ordering.

Ratings come from customers who bought the item. We do not solicit them and we do not remove the unflattering ones.
